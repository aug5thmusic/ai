MENUETTO & TRIO - INDIVIDUAL PARTS

Four one-page A4 parts:
  sq-minuet-violin-1.pdf
  sq-minuet-violin-2.pdf
  sq-minuet-viola.pdf
  sq-minuet-cello.pdf

Each page contains both Menuetto and Trio, including the da capo direction.
No page turns are required. Print each part at actual size (100%) on A4
paper to retain the engraved 20-point staff size.
Movement headings and the gap between sections use compact, fixed spacing,
leaving the available page height for spacing between musical systems.

The music matches the approved full score. Consecutive full-measure rests
are combined into counted rests. System breaks and tuplet placement have
been adjusted for individual reading. All pitches, durations, dynamics,
articulations, slurs, ornaments, tremolos, repeats, and endings are retained.

Bar numbers restart at the Trio and match the full score. Written numbering
counts both alternate endings: Trio 12/13 and 31/32. There are 28 written
measures in the Menuetto and 32 in the Trio.

EDITABLE SOURCE
sq-minuet-parts.ly is self-contained. With LilyPond 2.26.0 installed, run:
  lilypond sq-minuet-parts.ly
This creates all four named PDFs in the current directory.

The part source is an independent copy of the approved music; future edits
to the full-score source will not automatically update this parts file.
See engraving-notes.txt for the original scan and editorial readings.

VERIFICATION
All eight movement/part definitions were compared with the approved score,
expanding counted rests for the comparison. All musical content matches.
Individual measure and bar-number checks pass. LilyPond compiles without
warnings, and every final part page has been visually inspected.
